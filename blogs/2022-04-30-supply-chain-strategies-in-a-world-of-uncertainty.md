Organizations are struggling to deal with the paradigm shift happening in the manufacturing supply chain sector. We have: Increases in commodity volatility with Copper up 90%+ from 2020 Increases in frequency and severity of supply chain risks Growing geopolitical disruptions In this new paradigm; it is critical for businesses to have an effective supply chain strategy that adapts to the chaos of uncertainty.
